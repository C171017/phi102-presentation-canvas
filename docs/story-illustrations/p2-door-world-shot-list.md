## Earlier nine-shot plan

The notes and scripted prompts below record the version prepared before the trailer stills were supplied.

Nine pictures for the P2 door-world slides. They borrow the look and a handful of story beats of *Suzume* (2022) and replace every particular with our own. Each entry gives the film beat in words, what we draw instead, the framing, the acting, the light, the alt text and the prompt the generator sends.

- **Generator:** `scripts/generate-door-world-art.mjs` (`npm run art:door-world`; dry run `npm run art:door-world:check`). The prompts below are printed from that script. Edit them there and re-run `node scripts/generate-door-world-art.mjs --check --prompts` to see the result.
- **Model and size:** `gpt-image-2.6-sunburst`, quality `high`, `1536x1024` through `/v1/images/edits`. Each PNG is centre-cropped to 16:9 (1536 × 864) and saved as WebP, the same shape as the P1 art. The prompts keep faces and props out of the top and bottom tenth for that crop, and keep the lowest quarter quiet for the dialogue box.
- **Output:** `public/generated/backgrounds/ink-ocean/<id>.webp`, with `P2-door-world.manifest.json` beside them. The untouched PNGs stay in `docs/story-illustrations/door-world-raw/`.
- **Slide fitting:** show every frame with `contain`, at its native 16:9. P2-1 and P2-3 share one framing. P2-5, P2-6 and P2-7 share one set.

## Rules these prompts follow

1. **Look, in words.** Luminous, painterly Japanese feature animation: towering cumulus skies, sparkling sea light, soft lens glow, overgrown ruins, flooded floors with clear reflections, warm light against cool shadow. The prompts name no artist, studio or film, and tell the model to imitate none.
2. **Nothing from the film itself.** None of its characters, its cat, its chair, its title or logo, and no frame of it. Every composition here differs from its source beat in camera, setting and staging; the "Ours" line in each entry says how.
3. **Our cast.** Identity lines follow the current Ink Ocean designs. Acting comes from the [character bible](../character-bible.md): its prompt blocks, with the bracket filled in for the shot. Simone stays calm and open, Cole friendly, Echo half-lidded with one claw state.
4. **References are for identity only.** By default the generator attaches the current canvas figures in `src/assets/full-body-cast/`, one neutral figure per character, so there is no off-tone panel to copy. `--refs=version-c` attaches the Version C model sheets instead and adds a line telling the model to ignore the two banned Simone panels (the glare at the watch and the chin-on-hand slump).
5. **Ink-world rules.** Everything is ink except the hinge. Doors are dried black ink. The question marks are tiny black glyphs. No other writing appears; the lines on the hinge are too small to read.
6. **Continuity.** P2-3 is given the finished P2-1, P2-2c the finished P2-2b, P2-6 the finished P2-5 and P2-7 the finished P2-6. Generate in order. The script stops if an earlier frame it needs is missing.

## Shots

### 1. The road meeting · `P2-1-road-meeting.webp`

- **Film beat (for reference):** The film’s first meeting. A schoolgirl freewheels downhill on her bicycle along a coast road on a bright morning and passes a young traveller walking up. She brakes, he asks whether there are any ruins nearby because he is looking for a door, and she is visibly struck by him.
- **Ours:** Both travel uphill and it is the walker who turns round. The camera stands on the inland verge and looks across the road to the sea, so the two figures sit left and right of centre with open road between them.
