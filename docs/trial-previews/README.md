# P1 Trial visual previews

The [handheld pixel-art set](pixel-art/README.md) is the current Canvas artwork. Among the earlier HD-style studies, **Ivory and Brass** came closest to the HD Trilogy reference through its larger portraits, smoother fills, and plain cream-and-gold wall panels.

Each HD board shows the witness stand, judge’s bench, prosecutor’s desk, and a wide view in reading order. The wide view shows the room’s layout.

The style review compared Simone’s profile and the judge’s bench to the supplied HD Trilogy frames. [The attorney shot at 4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qx5BV__S3DE&t=242s), [the judge shot at 6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qx5BV__S3DE&t=411s), [Capcom’s Trilogy screenshots](https://store.steampowered.com/app/787480/Phoenix_Wright_Ace_Attorney_Trilogy/).

## Built-in ImageGen prompts

### A · Ivory and Brass

Use case: style-transfer. Create an ORIGINAL HD 2D 2000s Japanese courtroom visual-novel art preview for the original story Ink Ocean: three character cuts and one wide. The two gameplay screenshots are examples of old commercial sprite finish ONLY. Take no defined asset from either screenshot: no game characters, sprite poses, costumes, props, courtroom ornament, emblems, camera geometry, text or UI. Compose a new original Victorian lakeside municipal court, with cream plaster, brass inlaid sun rays, dark walnut, and light blue glimpses of the lake. The author’s original character lineup and gavel image supply the visual identities to draw.

Critical rendering treatment: faithfully evoke the finish of a first-generation Japanese handheld visual novel redrawn in HD. Extremely economical linework, clean fully filled black outlines, varied contour pressure, simple continuous curving anatomy, one thin interior contour per feature, one warm low-contrast shadow color with broad smoothly airbrushed blended patches for round cheek, finger, forehead and sleeve volumes. The characters are glossy and smooth like 2000s digital TV animation, not angular or gritty. Use very few lines on fabric, big single or two-tone fields of forest green, navy and plum. Make the characters look like clean individually hand-inked, airbrushed mid-2000s 2D game sprites. Plain big light ivory wall planes, shiny gold strips, hard 3D-like architectural bevels and simple shadow bands. No flecked paper, brush marks, facets, detailed wood grain, dramatic rim light or painterly contrast. Push visual readability close to the screenshots’ technique: sprites fill roughly 76 percent of each closeup and stand sharply apart from a simple smooth room. Reuse the same setting palette and light direction, not the same scenery composition. One chest-up character staged slightly off-center in each closeup with limited headroom. No dialogue box or interface.

A four-panel 2×2 horizontal contact sheet with equal size 16:9 quadrants, one very thin white cross exactly centered, no margin, each quadrant a complete crop. Top left: chest-up Simone, the leftmost woman in the character lineup, in her green gold-trimmed jacket, cream shirt, black ribbon tie, high looped jet-black ponytail with outward flick and side bang; preserve her face shape and green color. Upright relaxed shoulders, clear attentive eyes and relaxed brows, soft closed mouth. She faces slightly right with hands resting freely on walnut rail. Tiny Echo with a purplish conical shell and coral claw sits on the rail, half-lidded and one raised claw. Three simple panels of cream and gold behind her, a single arched window with light blue glass. Top right: a new original older judge with side-parted grey waves, smooth brown face, a narrow high nose and long clean-shaven chin, navy judicial robe and short gold stole. Tall blue and gold zodiac wheel behind him, low wood bench beneath him with our brass octagon and teal stripe gavel. Bottom left: original middle-aged prosecutor with dark brown swept-back hair, narrow round glasses, and plum coat over ocher waistcoat. Behind him polished gold-framed benches and his older mother in a bonnet, each rendered very simply. Bottom right: wide of the same cream limestone and honey gold 1890s lakeside courtroom, bench, two tables, audience. Distinct original designs from the example screenshots. No handcuffs, shackles, restraints, chains, watermarks, labels, text, or overlays.

### Simone speaker sprite

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single original Simone waist-up speaker sprite with true transparent alpha, for the original Ink Ocean courtroom parody. Reference 1 (gameplay screenshot) guides rendering technique ONLY: crisp black variable ink outlines, decisively simple expression, large smooth blocks of muted shadow and softly airbrushed peach highlights on the skin and slate highlights across cloth, a vintage HD-redrawn 2000s Japanese anime sprite finish. Keep only these broad visual techniques. Do not include any character, background, pose details, clothing, UI, logo, text, props, or any asset from image 1. Reference 2 is the Ink Ocean character lineup; use the far-left woman Simone only, identity not rendering technique. Reference 3 is a cropped original character model sheet for her posture and silhouette. Her high looped jet-black ponytail has one long tip curling outward and her side-swept bangs are sharp, recognizable in silhouette. She is young-adult, attentive and calm, with a soft closed mouth and a tiny half smile, level brows, bright catchlight, full dark eyes, relaxed shoulders. Preserve green fitted cropped jacket with fine gold piping and brass buttons, cream collared shirt, separate black ribbon tie and simple brown diagonal carrier strap. Waist-up, turns three-quarters to image-right looking a little above the viewer, left hand lightly holding the strap, right relaxed free hand partially visible by the waist. Keep both arms clear, all of the hair and shoulders inside the frame. Draw continuously curved anatomy, a softly rounded face, fine carefully tapered black ink and large smooth areas of flat forest green with gentle directional gradient shadows. Not faceted paper art, no paper grain, no busy hair strokes, no manga hatching, no background color, no outlined sticker border, no glow. Her expression is thoughtful with the faintest smile, never grumpy or glaring. Directly deliver one complete isolated waist-up sprite with transparent alpha. No restraint or jewelry on wrists, no furniture, no embedded reference, no text and no game UI.

Use condensed slab serif lettering and a cream, navy, and brass palette.

### Canonical Gavel

Create an original 1890s judicial gavel on transparent alpha in crisp two-dimensional cel shading. Give it a honey-gold brass head with beveled octagonal end faces, a thin teal enamel band, and an engraved eight-point star. Use a tapered walnut handle and a round reddish walnut sounding block, with a clear transparent gap between the hammer and block for the existing Canvas strike.
